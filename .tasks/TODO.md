# Gutter

Fix the Answer Table's selected-row border, then introduce the Gutter: a
reserved left column on every Set-page section at the wide window, one shared
width across the page, where structural marks live — Question labels, the
Answer Table's radio-and-number column, the Diff's line numbers — with content
starting at its right edge so all reading shares one left axis. Design settled
in a grilling session; the term is defined in CONTEXT.md.
