# Answering conveniences

The daily-driver polish on top of the answering web UI: one tap accepts every
Recommendation, a half-finished answer survives a page close, a dead agent is
visible at a glance, and answered or orphaned Question Sets live in a browsable
Archive.

Everything here is one human answering Sets from a phone, so the bias is
towards fewer taps and no surprises: nothing is ever pre-selected on load, a
draft is per-device and never synced, and Liveness only ever tells the human
something — it never withdraws a Set on its own.
